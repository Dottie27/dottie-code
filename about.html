ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Dottie ♥

Learning. Building. Coding.

I'm learning how to build websites and applications while working toward becoming a full-stack developer.

My Story

I studied Graphic Design in high school, where I discovered how much I enjoyed creating and bringing ideas to life through design. At the time, however, I wasn’t interested in the coding side of things. I loved designing websites and other creative projects, but I didn’t want to dive into the technical side of building them. As I’ve grown and learned more about technology, my perspective has changed. I’ve become curious about what happens behind the designs I create and how code can turn those ideas into something functional. Now, I’m stepping outside of my comfort zone and learning how to code. I’m exploring HTML, CSS, Python, and more as I work toward becoming a full-stack developer. Dottie Codes is a place where I can document that journey, share what I’m learning, and build projects along the way.

What I'm Building

One of my projects is WalkWise, a walking calculator designed to help users understand how much and how long they should walk based on their goals.

I'm using projects like WalkWise to practice programming, problem-solving, and eventually connecting a front end with Python.
