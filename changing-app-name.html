Changing My App Name: From WalkWise to MyStep Journey

October 2026

Today, I started planning my fitness app and was getting ready to begin writing the HTML code to bring my idea to life. Before jumping into the coding, though, I had a feeling that I should check whether the name I had chosen, WalkWise, was already being used.

Unfortunately, it was!

I discovered that WalkWise was already being used by an app focused on something similar to my idea, but designed for seniors.

Although I was disappointed, I realized that checking the name early was an important step before putting too much time into building the app.

A Tip for Anyone Starting a Project

If you are thinking about creating a website, an app, or any other project, here is something I recommend:

Don't get too attached to your first name idea!

When you come up with a name, you might love it right away. It sounds good, fits your idea, and feels like the perfect choice. However, before settling on that name, there are a few things you should do.

Make a list of names

Write down as many ideas as you can think of. Don't be afraid to get creative!

Ask others for suggestions

Sometimes, another person can think of a name you would never have considered.

Use a temporary name

Pick a name to use as a placeholder while you work on your idea. Remember, you can always change it later.

Do your research

Look into similar apps, websites, or projects to see what is already out there.

Check the name before committing

Search online and check relevant app stores, websites, and business name records. If someone is already using your preferred name, you may need to consider another option.

Taking these steps early can save you time and help you avoid having to rename your project later.

Finding a New Name

Since WalkWise was already being used, I had to go back to the drawing board and brainstorm some new ideas.

Some of the names I considered were:

  • Daily Steps
  • 1, 2 Steps
  • StepApp

These were just a few of the ideas I explored, but I discovered that some of these names were already being used, too.

It was back to brainstorming!

After considering different options, I came up with a new name that I really liked:

MyStep Journey

I checked the Apple App Store and Google Play Store and, at the time of my search, I didn't find an app using that exact name. That was encouraging!

However, I still need to do more research to make sure the name is available for me to use.

Moving Forward With MyStep Journey

Now that I have a new name to work with, I can start focusing on bringing my idea to life.

MyStep Journey is going to be a fitness app centered around walking and tracking personal fitness progress. I am excited to start building it and see how the idea develops as I learn more about coding.

This is another reminder that creating something new doesn't always go exactly as planned. Sometimes, you have to make changes along the way, do a little more research, and find a different direction.

Even though I had to let go of the name WalkWise, I see this as another learning experience.

Building a project isn't just about writing code. It's also about planning, researching, solving problems, and learning as you go.

I'm looking forward to continuing my journey with MyStep Journey and sharing my progress along the way!
